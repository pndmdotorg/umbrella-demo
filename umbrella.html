HOW IT WORKS ×

MONEY IN

You pay USDG (on Robinhood Chain) to buy a bond. The bond is minted as a Virus NFT — an ERC-6551 token-bound account. Bond price follows the Olympus debt-ratio formula; the discount to market UMBRELLA is your edge for locking capital. A purchase fee is taken in USDG at buy time.

TREASURY BUYS PFIZER

Your USDG is split: a remittance to Core Treasury, the rest buys tokenized PFE via the Robinhood Stock Token route. Atomic path: PFE lands in the RWA Sleeve in the same transaction. Fallback: USDG goes to the Sleeve and a Safe buys PFE later. The Sleeve is a separate Safe — PFE never enters reserve accounting and cannot mint UMBRELLA. PFE price comes from a Chainlink Stock Token feed with staleness, pause and range checks; the atomic path pauses when the feed is stale (outside market hours).

THE VIRUS IS THE BOND AND THE STAKE

The NFT's token-bound account holds the UMBRELLA payout and everything that accrues to it. Each rebase credits yield inside the NFT — only while it is staked in the pool. The NFT is freely transferable: selling it sells principal plus accrued yield as one unit.

CLAIM BURNS THE VIRUS

Claim is one-shot: you receive principal plus yield minus the claim fee, and the NFT is destroyed. The fee depends on how many holders claim in the same one-minute window — the World Alert on the map shows the current fee, and it resets every minute. Crowded exits cost more. Half the fee is burned; half is redistributed to the NFTs still staked.

WHAT BACKS WHAT

UMBRELLA is minted only against Core Treasury excess reserves. PFE in the Sleeve is protocol-owned equity exposure, not backing; how its value flows back to Virus holders is not yet defined.

This is a simulation running in your browser — no wallet, no chain. Epochs are accelerated: one rebase every seconds stands in for an 8-hour epoch.
